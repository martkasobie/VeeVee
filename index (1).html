Watch Party Guide & Troubleshooting 🍿

1. Clean Top vs Bottom Bars (No Duplicate Buttons!)

Hover near the bottom edge of the movie for all playback controls (Start/Pause, Seekbar, Volume, Audio & Subtitles, Watch Hub, Stop Stream). Hover near the top edge in Full Screen for Social & Hardware controls (Mic, Cam, Emojis, Laser, PiP, Exit Full).

2. Audio & Subtitles Across All Platforms (v5.1)

Click Audio & Subtitles (CC) on the bottom video bar, then click Scan Platform Languages to remotely switch audio/subtitles on Netflix, Disney+, HBO Max, or Prime Video, or upload your own .srt file!

3. Camera or Microphone Not Showing?

Click Mic On or Cam On (or the arrow icon next to Mic) to re-request permissions from Chrome and pick your physical webcam/microphone.
