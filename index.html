Watch Party Guide & Troubleshooting 🍿

1. Netflix Error M7375 or Black Screen?

Our v3.0 Extension uses Netflix's official Cadmium API so it never triggers M7375. If Netflix cached an older error, visit netflix.com/clearcookies once. For black screens when sharing, disable "Use graphics acceleration when available" in Chrome Settings → System.

2. Independent Subtitles & Audio Languages

Click the Audio & Subtitles (CC) button on the video control bar to load your own .srt or .vtt subtitles (visible only to you) or switch the stream's spoken audio track.

3. Camera or Microphone Not Showing?

Click Mic On or Cam On (or the arrow icon next to Mic) to re-request permissions from Chrome and pick your physical webcam/microphone.
