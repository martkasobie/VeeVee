How to Watch Together?

1. Open Netflix, Disney+, Max or YouTube in another tab

Use Chrome, Edge, or Brave and open your streaming site, or pick a local MP4 file directly from your computer.

2. Click "Share Screen / Tab" & control right from the video

Enable "Also share tab audio". Hover over the video stage anytime to Pause/Resume, Change Source, or Stop Sharing!

3. Seeing a black screen on Netflix / Disney+?

Disable "Use graphics acceleration when available" in your browser settings (Settings → System) — this prevents DRM black screens.
